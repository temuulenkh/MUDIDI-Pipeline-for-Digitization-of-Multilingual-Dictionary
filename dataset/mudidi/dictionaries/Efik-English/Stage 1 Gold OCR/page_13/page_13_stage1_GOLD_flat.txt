Art; Äll.—Met; Thëre.—Ravine; Bid.—Ñ=ng.
<b>DÏM'-E</b>, <i>v.</i>, <b>DUM'-E</b>, <i>v. tr.</i> To make
sonorous, big, as the voice.
Odïme uyu ke itöñ, <i>He makes his</i>
<i>voice big in his throat.</i>
<b>DÏM'-E</b>, <i>v. intr.</i> To resound, be son-
orous, noisy. Ekömö odïme ke
ufök, <i>The drum makes a big noise</i>
<i>in the house.</i> Uyu odïme ke itöñ,
<i>His voice is sonorous.</i> Odïme
anam utüm, <i>He works strongly</i>,
or, <i>with a bustle.</i>
Der. <i>n.</i>, Udïme-ntïbi.
<b>DI-ÖK'-HÖ, III.,</b> <i>v. tr.</i> (Diök. I.) To
irritate, incense. Ikä orü ödiökhö
mi ke ësët, <i>That word irritates me.</i>
Ödiökhö fi ke ësët ye ami, <i>It</i>
<i>incenses you against me.</i> Others,
To surfeit.
<b>DI-ÖN</b>, <i>v. tr. & intr.</i> To climb. <i>Ib.</i>,
<i>Ef.</i>, Düñ.
<b>DÖB'-Ö</b>. See Däbä.
<b>DÖÑ</b>, <i>v. tr.</i> 6. With inua. To inter-
pose. Mö ëdöñ inua kësët, <i>They</i>
<i>object to the thing.</i> Others, <i>They</i>
<i>give a charge concerning it.</i>
Der. <i>n.</i>, Udön̄-inua, <i>v.</i> ikä.
<b>DÖÑ</b>, <i>v. tr. Ib.</i>, Döñ akam, <i>To pray.</i>
<i>Ef.</i>, Böñ.
<b>DU-Ä'</b>, <i>v. intr.</i> 4. To come far down,
as a garment; to be flowing.
5. To fall out, result in, as a divi-
nation. Idiöñ öduä owo ke ëbüd,
<i>v.</i>, unen, <i>Idiöñ directs the sacrifice</i>
<i>of a goat or fowl.</i>
<b>DU-AT'</b>, <i>v. intr.</i> 3. To chatter,
perhaps loudly in anger. Spoken
by some of the monkey.
<b>DÜB'-O</b>, <i>v. tr.</i> To silence, quiet.
Odübo uyüm, <i>He silences the noise.</i>
Odübo eköñ, <i>He quiets the strife,</i>
<i>warfare.</i>
<b>DU-EN'-DU-EN</b>, <i>adv.</i> Smooth; shin-
ing, <i>e.g.</i>, any thing polished.
Etie duen-duen, <i>It is highly</i>
<i>polished.</i>
<b>DÜM'-O, II.</b> <i>v. tr.</i> 2. To burn. Mö
ëdümo adan, idümoke kande, <i>They</i>
<i>burn oil, not candles.</i>
<b>DU-OI'</b>, <i>v. intr.</i> 2. To fall or hang
down, as the end of a napkin
which should be tied up. Used
by some as <i>Syn.</i> of Kpöm.
<b>DU-ÖK'</b>, <i>v. tr.</i> 15. To overwhelm.
Mfat öduök enye, <i>Mfat quite</i>
<i>covers him.</i>
16. <i>Trop.</i> To shed, throw, as a
glittering object its lustre. Mba
esie öduök idañ, <i>v.</i>, möñ okuk,
<i>Its wing glistens like plating or</i>
<i>gilding.</i>
<b>DU-ÖK'-HÖ</b>, <i>v. intr. & ref.</i> 1. To
fail in accomplishing a purpose or
agreement.
2. To throw one's-self upon. Mö
ëduökhö ke udia, <i>They fall raven-</i>
<i>ously on the food.</i>
<b>DU-ÖÑ'-Ö</b>, <i>v. intr.</i> To lurch, as a
ship.
<b>DU-ÖT'</b>, <i>v. tr.</i> To make inert, feeble.
Idem öduöt mi, <i>I feel indisposed.</i>
<i>Syn.</i>, Mem.
<b>DU-ÖT'</b>, <i>v. intr.</i> To be heavy.
Spoken of rain. Edim öduöt,
<i>v.</i>, edep öduöt, <i>The rain falls</i>
<i>heavily.</i>
<b>DÜR'-O</b>, <i>v. tr.</i> To rest on. Odüro
ke mben, <i>It is brimful.</i>
<b>DUT, II.</b> <i>v. intr. & tr.</i> To be stringy,
tough, wiry. Unam odut, <i>The</i>
<i>beef is tough.</i> Idem odut enye, <i>v.</i>,
etie enye idut-idut, <i>His sinews are</i>
<i>prominent.</i>
Der. <i>a. & adv.</i>, Idut-idut, and
udut-udut.
<b>DU-TU'</b>, <i>v. tr.</i> To pick up, gather;
to collect one by one. Used by
some of small, by others of dis-
tant things.
Der. <i>n.</i> Ndutu.
<b>DU-TUK'-HÄ</b>, <i>v. intr.</i> To be afflicted,
tormented. Odutukhä eti-eti, <i>He</i>
<i>is much afflicted.</i>
