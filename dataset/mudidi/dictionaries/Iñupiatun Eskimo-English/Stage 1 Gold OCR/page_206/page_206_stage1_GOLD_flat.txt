taaŋŋaqti
taġiuġmiut
<b>imiqtuaq.</b> <i>See:</i> <b>imiġuuruaq</b>.
<b>taaŋŋaqti</b> <i>n.</i> alcoholic; drunkard. <i>Syn:</i> <b>imiġuuruaq</b>;
<b>imiŋaruaq</b>; <b>imiqtuaq</b>.
<b>taapkua</b> <i>dem.pron.</i> those right there (plural). <i>Syn:</i>
<b>taamna</b>.
<b>taapkunani</b> those located right there (locative).
<b>taapkunuŋa</b> toward those right there
(terminalis).
<b>taapkunakŋa</b> from those right there (ablative).
<i>Variant:</i> <b>taapkunaŋŋa</b>.
<b>taapkunuuna</b> through those right there (vialis).
<b>taapkunatun</b> like those right there (similaris).
<b>taapkuniŋa</b> those right there, with those right
there (modalis).
<b>taapkuak</b> <i>dem.pron.</i> those two right there (dual).
<i>Syn:</i> <b>taamna</b>.
<b>taapkuŋnaŋni</b> those two located right there
(locative).
<b>taapkuŋnuŋa</b> toward those two right there
(terminalis).
<b>taapkuŋnakŋa</b> from those two right there
(ablative). <i>Variant:</i> <b>taapkuŋnaŋŋa</b>.
<b>taapkuŋnuuna</b> through those two right there
(vialis).
<b>taapkuŋnaktun</b> like those two right there
(similaris).
<b>taapkuŋniŋa</b> those two right there, with those
two right there (modalis).
<b>taaq</b> <i>n.</i> darkness.
<b>taaq-</b> <i>vi.</i> 1) to be dark. 2) to become dusky, to have
little light.
<b>taaqsaq-</b> <i>vt.</i> to darken hair. <i>Lit:</i> 'make darker'.
<i>Variant:</i> <b>taaqtitaq-</b>.
<b>taaqsi-</b> <i>vi.</i> to become dark.
<b>taaqsima-</b> <i>vi.</i> to be unadjusted to darkness (of eyes).
<b>taaqsipaiyaaq</b> <i>n.</i> 1) half-negro. 2) Negro child;
young Negro.
<b>taaqsipak</b> <i>n.</i> Negro (member of a major human racial
division traditionally distinguished by physical
characteristics such as brown to black
pigmentation and often tightly curled hair);
dark-skinned person.
<b>taaqsisuqsi-</b> <i>vi.</i> to become dark sooner.
<b>taaqsiulguit-</b> <i>vi.</i> to be unadjusted to darkness (of
eyes); to have difficulty seeing in the dark.
<b>taaqtaaq</b> <i>n.</i> something black; anything of a dark
color.
<b>taaqtaq</b> <i>n.</i> something black; blue denim.
<b>taaqtinniq</b> <i>n.</i> bruise. <i>Variant:</i> <b>anaqtinniq</b>.
<b>taaqtukkaq-</b> <i>vi.</i> to faint, to black out. <i>Syn:</i> <b>aiyaaŋa-</b>;
<b>imuliq-</b>; <b>kuniġaq-</b>; <b>tuquanŋu-</b>. <i>Variant:</i>
<b>taaqtupkaq-</b>.
<b>taaqtuliq-</b> <i>vi.</i> to turn dark earlier (of days in fall).
<b>taaqtupkaq-</b> <i>vi.</i> to faint. <i>See:</i> <b>aayaaŋa-</b>. <i>Variant:</i>
<b>taaqtukkaq-</b>.
<b>taaqtupkaġniala-</b> to have fainting spells.
<b>taaqtupkaqayaq-</b> to nearly nearly faint.
<b>taata</b> <i>n.</i> father. <i>Variant:</i> <b>aapa</b>.
<b>taataiḷaq</b> <i>n.</i> fatherless child, illegitimate child. <i>Lit:</i>
'one without a father'. <i>Variant:</i> <b>aapaiḷaq</b>.
<b>taataksraq</b> <i>n.</i> stepfather. <i>Variant:</i> <b>aapaksraq</b>.
<b>taataruaq</b> <i>n.</i> 1) grandfather. 2) great-uncle.
<b>taatchiŋiq</b> <i>n.</i> common loon. <i><u>Gavia immer<u/></i>.
<b>taatchiŋiraq</b> <i>n.</i> slate-colored junco. <i><u>Junco hyemalis</u></i>.
<b>taatna</b> <i>adv.</i> like this; thus, in this manner. <b>Taatnatun</b>
<b>umialik tuqulliñiaqtuq iñuuŋuraġniallaġmi</b>.
In the same way the rich man is going to die
while he goes about his life. <i>Variant:</i> <b>itna</b>.
<b>taatnaluasru</b> <i>excl.</i> word used when agreeing with
speaker. <i>Syn:</i> <b>asulu</b>.
<b>taatnałhaiññaq</b> <i>adv.</i> continually; that way all the
time; repeatedly in that manner.
<b>taatnamik</b> <i>adv.</i> therefore.
<b>taatnaqtit-</b> <i>vi.</i> to fulfill; to make happen. <i>Lit:</i> 'let it
become that way'.
<b>taatnasiq</b> <i>n.</i> certain kind; certain type, certain class.
<i>Variant:</i> <b>taatnasriq</b>.
<b>taatnasriq</b> <i>n.</i> certain kind; certain type, certain class.
<i>Variant:</i> <b>taatnasiq</b>.
<b>taatnaunnii</b> <i>excl.</i> do it that way anyway because it
will be acceptable.
<b>taatniuli</b> <i>excl.</i> "let it be"; leave (him, her, it) alone.
<b>taggiñ</b> <i>n.</i> 1) term (a name of something); a title of
something. <i>Syn:</i> <b>taiñiq</b>. 2) noun (in Iñupiat
grammar).
<b>tagiala-</b> <i>vi.</i> 1) to feel unwelcome. 2) to be homesick.
<b>tagialanait-</b> <i>vi.</i> to be hospitable (of an enjoyable
atmosphere); to feel welcomed. <i>Syn:</i> <b>niġiñaq-</b>;
<b>tukkii-</b>; <b>tutqiŋñaq-</b>.
<b>tagialanaq-</b> <i>vi.</i> to be inhospitable (of unfavorable
atmosphere).
<b>tagiaqtuq-</b> <i>vi.</i> to go away from home (not feeling
welcome anymore).
<b>tagiuq</b> <i>n.</i> worm which inhabits the nasal cavity
(especially of caribou).
<b>tagiuq-</b> <i>vi.</i> to sneeze. (Originally thought to be
caused by annoying the worm inside the nose.).
<b>taglu</b> <i>n.</i> snowshoe.
<b>tagraq-</b> <i>vi.</i> to travel upriver.
<b>taġġaq</b> <i>n.</i> shadow; a reflection.
<b>taġġaqtuq-</b> <i>vt.</i> to reflect, to mirror.
<b>taġġaqtuun</b> <i>n.</i> mirror.
<b>taġġiñiq</b> <i>n.</i> shade; shadow.
<b>taġġit-</b> <i>vi.</i> 1) to move into the shade or into a cool
place. 2) to disappear from sight into the
shadows. <i>See:</i> <b>maġġutliġaq-</b>. 3) to be thin (like
paper).
<b>taġiuġmiut</b> <i>n.</i> coast dwellers.
