<b>ܐܵܓ̰ܸܪ</b> âjir, <i>punishment, revenge, recompense for</i>
<i>evil</i>.
<b>ܐܲܓ݂ܝܼܪܵܐ</b> l-îrâ Al., OS. <b>ܐܲܓ݂ܝܼܪܵܐ</b> (see <b>ܐܵܓ݂ܪܵܐ</b>), m. i,

<b>ܐܲܓ݂ܝܼܪܬܵܐ</b> agirtâ, OS., f. 6, a <i>small coin</i>.

<i>lady, landlord's wife</i>.
<b>ܐܵܓ̰ܵܐܠ</b> âjâl, arab. turk. pers. اجل, f. i, <i>time of</i>
<i>death</i>.

a <i>haberdasher</i>.
<b>ܐܸܓ̰ܠܵܣ</b> ijlâs, arab. turk. اجلاس (fr. جلس
