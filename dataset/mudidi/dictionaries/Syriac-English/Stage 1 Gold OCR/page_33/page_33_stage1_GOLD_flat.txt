ܐܲܓ݂ܵܐ 10 ܐܸܓܸ݂ܪܵܐ
<b>ܐܲܓ݂ܵܐ</b> (—ܵܐ) [T. آقا] Mt. <i>m.</i> A lord;
a landlord; master.
<b>ܐܸܓ݂ܝܼܛ</b> [T. اگید] <i>m.</i> A Young man;
brave, heroic.
<b>ܐܲܓ݂ܝܼܪܵܐ</b> (—ܹܐ) Mt. <i>m.</i> Brave; rich;
smart in everything.
<b>ܐܸܕܝܘܿܡ</b> 2 <i>adv.</i> To-day (an erroneous
pronunciation of the word <b>ܗܵܕܝܼܘܼܡ</b>).
<b>ܐܲܓ݂ܵܝܘܼܬ݂ܵܐ</b> (—ܵܬ݂ܹܐ) Lordship, master-
ship.
<b>ܐܸܓܸ݂ܠ</b> (—ܹܐ) [P. آغیل] <i>m.</i> A sheep-
cot, sheep-fold.
<b>ܐܲܓ݂ܵܝܵܢܹܐ</b> [T. P. آقایانه] <i>adv.</i> Masterly; gal-
lantly, nobly.
<b>ܐܲܓ݂ܵܝܵܢܘܼܬ݂ܵܐ</b> Gallantry; nobleness.
<b>ܐܵܓ݂ܝܼܨ ܦܸܣܩܝܼ</b> [T. آجی قیجی lit. ‘bitter
herb’] <i>f.</i> In such a phrase as,
<b>ܡܵܘܬܸܒ݂ܠ ܐܵܓ݂ܝܼܨ ܦܸܣܩܝܼ</b> ‘to gall one’s
enemies’.
<b>ܐܸܓ̰ܝܼܪܵܐ</b> (—ܹܐ) [A. اجير] <i>m.</i> The mal-
treatment or abuse which a slave or
a hired laborer is liable to encounter
(the plural form is more common).
<b>ܐܸܓ̰ܝܼܪܘܼܬ݂ܵܐ</b> (—ܵܬ݂ܹܐ) [cs.] Alk. <i>m.</i> A mer-
cenary; a hired laborer, a hireling
(euphonically spelled also <b>ܐܸܓ̰ܝܼܪܘܼܬ݂ܵܐ</b>).
<b>ܐܲܓ݂ܪܝܼܕ݂ܵܐܓ݂</b>, <b>ܐܲܓ݂ܪܝܼܕ݂ܵܐܚ</b> [T. آغرداغ] <i>m.</i> Mount
Ararat.
<b>ܐܸܓ̰ܝܼܪܬ݂ܵܐ</b> (—ܵܬ݂ܹܐ) <i>f.</i> A woman hireling.
<b>ܐܸܓ̰ܪܵܐ</b> [cs.] <i>f.</i> A piece of silver;
earnest money; reward.
<b>ܐܲܓ̰ܵܝܬ݂ܵܐ</b> (—ܵܬ݂ܹܐ) A lady; the feminine
of the title <b>ܐܲܓ݂ܵܐ</b>.
<b>ܐܲܓ݂ܙܵܐ</b>, (—ܹܐ, —ܵܬ݂ܵܐ) [T. اوکچه] <i>f.</i>
Heel, A pivot or spur of a door.
See <b>ܡܲܫܪܵܐ</b>.
<b>ܐܸܓ̰ܸܠ</b> [A. اجل] <i>f.</i> The hour of death,
the predestined time, death; doom.
<b>ܐܸܓ݂ܠܸܒ</b> [A. اغلب] <i>adj.</i> Most, the greater
part.
<b>ܐܸܓ݂ܠܲܒܵܐ</b>, <b>ܐܲܓ݂ܠܲܒܵܐܝܼܬ݂</b> [A. اغلباً] <i>ult. adv.</i>
Chiefly, mostly.
<b>ܐܲܓ݂ܠܲܒܸܢܓ̰ܝܼ</b> (—ܹܐ) [A. P. اقلّبند] <i>m.</i> A
maker or seller of notions, a
haberdasher.
<b>ܐܸܓ̰ܠܵܘܿܬ݂ܵܐ</b> (—ܹܐ) [k.] <i>m.</i> A frying pan.
<b>ܐܸܓ̰ܠܵܣܚܵܢܵܐ</b> (—ܹܐ) [A. اجلاس] <i>f.</i> A com-
mittee, council; an assembly; a
session; congress.
<b>ܐܲܓ̰ܲܠܵܐ</b> [A. عجلة] TiA. <i>f.</i> A carriage,
cart, wagon; Lidz. S. S. 179. 12.
Comp. <b>ܥܲܓ̰ܲܠܵܐ</b>.
<b>ܐܲܓ̰ܲܡ</b> [A. عجم] <i>n.</i> and <i>adj.</i> Persia, a
Persian; a barbarian (more cor-
rectly <b>ܥܲܓ̰ܲܡ</b>).
<b>ܐܸܓܸ݂ܪܵܐ</b> (—ܹܐ) [cs.] Mt. A water spring.
