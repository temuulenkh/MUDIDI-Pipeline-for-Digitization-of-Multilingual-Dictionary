--- Accent Marks (Tones & Vowel Harmony) ---
` (Grave accent) - Indicates a Low tone (e.g., à, è, ò)
´ (Acute accent) - Indicates a High tone (e.g., á, é, ó)
ˆ (Circumflex) - Indicates a Falling tone (e.g., â, ê, ô)
¨ (Diaeresis) - Indicates a breathy or centralized vowel (ä, ï, ö). Note: While ¨ is an accent mark, standard Shilluk orthography treats ä, ï, and ö as completely separate letters in the alphabet rather than modified base vowels.
